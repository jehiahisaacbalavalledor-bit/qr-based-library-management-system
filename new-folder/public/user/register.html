Student Registration Guide

Please review the registration guidelines before signing up:

  • Student Number: Enter your official 9-digit CvSU Student Number (e.g., 202410123 or 2024-10123).
  • Email Address: Preferably your university email or an active personal email for borrowing notices & overdue reminders.
  • Mobile Number: Must be an active 11-digit Philippine mobile number starting with 09 (e.g., 09123456789) for SMS return alerts.
  • Date of Birth: Used for identity verification and account recovery.
  • Password: Must be at least 6 characters long. Keep your password confidential.
