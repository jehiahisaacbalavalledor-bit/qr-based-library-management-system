CvSU Imus Campus Library Support

Welcome to the Cavite State University - Imus Campus Library Book Borrowing Management System support desk.

  • Library Hours: Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Email Helpdesk: library.imus@cvsu.edu.ph
  • Inquiry Hotline: (046) 471-6607
  • Location: 2nd Floor, Main Academic Building, Imus, Cavite

For account issues, lost Student IDs, or QR borrowing card concerns, visit the circulation counter with your valid school ID.

System Walkthrough & Guide

How to Borrow Books with QR:

  1. Log in using your registered Student Number and Password.
  2. View your unique Student QR Library Pass on your dashboard.
  3. Browse or search available library catalog books.
  4. Present your digital QR code at the circulation kiosk/counter scanner to instantly check out or return books.
